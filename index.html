what does it do?

how it connects

AI coding tools can’t see file sizes, so 4 MB photos slip into your project. AssetDiet plugs in and does the checking for them.

1 · askyou tell your AI to check your media.
2 · scanAssetDiet lists the heavy files and the savings.
3 · shrinka dedicated engine compresses each file type.

four steps. zero guessing.

drag the notes around, they don’t mind.

1 · scanhash files, link code refs
2 · plansafe, balanced or aggressive
3 · optimizenative engines, temp files
4 · verifyatomic swap, or discard
